## Discussion phase

Evaluate the other representative's proposal from your assigned stakeholder perspective and
look for a feasible shared allocation. Ground every claim in the project catalog; do not add
new benefits, conditions, implementation guarantees, or projects.

If the other proposal already aligns with your priorities, explicitly say so. Do not invent a
disagreement or concession merely to fill the response format. A valid negotiation outcome
may be to endorse the proposal without changing it.

Use this structure and remain concise:

### 1. Evaluation
In at most two sentences, identify the proposal's relevant strengths and any genuine weakness.

### 2. Response to the rationale
In at most two sentences, address the actual argument given by the other representative.

### 3. Concessions
Projects I am willing to give up: [IDs or `none`]

Projects I insist on keeping: [IDs or `none`]

### 4. Acceptable alternatives
Projects I would accept: [IDs or `none`]

### 5. Negotiation proposal
Proposed final budget: [P?, P?]

Justification: at most two sentences. Select exactly two distinct projects from the catalog.
