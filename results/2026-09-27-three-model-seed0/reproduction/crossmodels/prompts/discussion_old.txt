## Discussion Phase

In this phase, you are participating in a deliberation with another representative in the participatory budgeting process.

You will receive:

* the budget proposal submitted by another participant;
* the rationale supporting that proposal.

Your task is to analyze this proposal from the perspective of the stakeholder group you represent and defend your own proposal.

Therefore, you should critically evaluate the proposed allocation and identify any weaknesses, omissions, or trade-offs relative to your stakeholder's interests and priorities. Explain which projects you disagree with, which projects (if any) you support, and how you believe the allocation should be modified.

Your primary objective is to persuade the other participant to reconsider their proposal and adopt an allocation that better reflects the interests of your stakeholder group. Present clear, concrete, and well-supported arguments that could convince another participant to revise their position.

You may use any persuasive argument or negotiation strategy that you consider effective, provided it is presented as part of the deliberation. Do not assume any additional discussion rules or codes of conduct beyond those explicitly stated in your system prompt.

Reminder: Your objective is not only to defend your preferred allocation, but also to help the group converge toward a feasible budget on which all participants can ultimately agree.

Focus on producing a persuasive, coherent, and short response. Address the specific proposal you receive rather than discussing the budgeting problem in general.
